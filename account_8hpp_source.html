1// axiam account lifecycle and MFA enrolment — CONTRACT.md §25.
2//
3// The calls a user makes about their own account, none of which is
4// administration: voluntary and forced TOTP enrolment, email verification, and
5// the password-reset triple. All nine have been live server surface since before
6// §1 was written; what they lacked was an SDK, which is exactly the divergence
7// §1 exists to prevent, arrived at through omission.
8//
9// SIX OF THE NINE ARE DELIBERATELY UNAUTHENTICATED. A user who cannot log in is
10// the entire audience for a password reset, and a user whose email is unverified
11// may have no session at all.
12//
13// WHERE THE TENANT GOES. `verify_email`, `resend_verification` and
14// `confirm_password_reset` take it as a BODY field — these are not `/oauth2`
15// endpoints, so §12.1 rule 2's query-parameter convention does not reach them.
16// `request_password_reset` accepts the workspace in slug form as well, like
17// login.
