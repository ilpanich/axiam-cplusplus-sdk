◆ effect

std::optional<PermissionEffect> axiam::management::GrantPermissionRequest::effect = std::nullopt

B1: "allow" (the default) or "deny".

A deny grant overrides every allow, at any depth of the resource hierarchy and at equal specificity — it is not most-specific-wins. See claude_dev/deny-override-design.md for the precedence table and for why that trade was made. Omitting the field means "allow", so every existing client keeps working unchanged and no migration is required. Optional.
