Detailed Description

Registered OAuth2/OIDC clients – the registration half of what §12, §21 and §26 then speak to.

The oauth2_clients namespace handle (§27.2), reached as client.management().oauth2_clients(). §27.3's C++ row is client.service_accounts().rotate_secret(id) – a method returning a handle, snake_case – and that is what this is.

Every method goes through the one shared transport, so §3 CSRF, the §4 cookie jar, the §5 tenant header, §6 TLS, §16 retry and §19 telemetry apply without this class doing anything to opt in (§27.8).
