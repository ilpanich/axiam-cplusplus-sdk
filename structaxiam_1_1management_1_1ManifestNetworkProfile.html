Detailed Description

The network.profiles[] spec (CONTRACT.md §27.6.2 item 1), for an entity of kind NetworkProfile.

A profile has no name or id of its own: its natural key is its segment.

Judged on the read (§27.6.2 item 3). A stated member is compared with the effective profile the read returns, and session_timeout_secs with the effective – clamped – value. A value above the tenant cap therefore differs from the read and plans as an Update; apply sends the replace_profile, the server refuses it (403 authorization_denied), and that is the action's failure: reported as given, apply stops, nothing is retried or lowered to the cap by the SDK.
