6// §27's management surface is the one part deliberately NOT pulled in here.
7// It is 147 operations and their model types — around five thousand lines of
8// declarations — and most programs that talk to AXIAM authenticate and check
9// access without ever administering a tenant. Making every translation unit pay
10// for it would be a compile-time tax on the common case, so it is opt-in:

12// #include "axiam/management.hpp" // namespace handles + models
13// #include "axiam/management_manifest.hpp" // §27.6/§27.7 declarative layer

15// Both are fully public and installed; `client.management()` is declared in
16// client.hpp either way, so the umbrella alone is enough to see that the surface

20// Refuse a toolchain older than the declared floor at the point of inclusion.
