The RFC 8935 §2.4 err to answer a push with (or to pass in a poll's setErrs).

The reason itself for invalid_key, invalid_issuer, invalid_audience and invalid_request; **invalid_request for malformed, invalid_type and replayed**, which RFC 8935 does not define.

◆ kMinReplayWindow

constexpr std::chrono::seconds axiam::ssf::kMinReplayWindow {7 * 24 * 60 * 60}
inlineconstexpr

The replay window's default AND floor: seven days, the transmitter's buffer retention (§32.6).

A shorter window would forget a jti the transmitter can still re-send, so one is refused at construction rather than shortened.
