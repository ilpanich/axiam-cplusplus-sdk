Detailed Description

Users within the client's tenant, and the administrative side of their second factor and lockout state.

The users namespace handle (§27.2), reached as client.management().users(). §27.3's C++ row is client.service_accounts().rotate_secret(id) – a method returning a handle, snake_case – and that is what this is.

Every method goes through the one shared transport, so §3 CSRF, the §4 cookie jar, the §5 tenant header, §6 TLS, §16 retry and §19 telemetry apply without this class doing anything to opt in (§27.8).
