◆ PermissionEffect

Whether a grant permits an action or refuses it (B1, deny-override).

Precedence

Default deny -> an [PermissionEffect::Allow] grant permits -> a [PermissionEffect::Deny] grant refuses, and beats every allow, wherever either sits in the resource hierarchy. Deny wins; there is no most-specific-wins tie-break.

That choice is deliberate and is argued in full in claude_dev/deny-override-design.md §2.1. [PermissionEffect::Allow] is the default, so data written before this existed, and clients that send no effect, both mean "allow". No migration.
