Detailed Description

The CONTRACT.md §27 management surface: 147 operations across 24 namespaces.

Reached as client.management(). Each accessor hands back a namespace handle (§27.2) that can be re-scoped per call with in_org() / for_tenant().

Handles are constructed on demand rather than cached. §27.4 rule 10 forbids caching RESPONSES, not handles – but a handle is a shared pointer and a scope, and building one per call keeps in_org() from having anything shared to mutate.
